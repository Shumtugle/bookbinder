# Changelog

## 0.8.1
- A new icon: a flat open book, its spine stitched, lines of text on one page and the levels of a sound on the other, with a silhouette for themed icons.

## 0.8.0
- The shelf puts the book opened or heard last on top, even if it was only opened.
- Every book on the shelf shows a wide bar of the whole book, cut into its chapters; the place within a chapter now counts by time once the chapter has been played.
- The place in a chapter is a wide strip with a drum: hold or drag it, and a drum of five-second ticks rises under a needle, with the time and the distance from now; near the finger it moves a tick at a time, further off faster and faster, held at an edge it keeps turning, and lifting the finger goes there.

## 0.7.1
- A book from the shelf folders is remembered again after the player has stopped: the widget waits for the folders to be looked through instead of showing an empty shelf, and the player opens the book as soon as it is found, keeping a play key pressed meanwhile.

## 0.7.0
- Looks: three palettes (paper, a warm theme, the wallpaper's own colours on Android 12 and later), each light or dark, and corners sharp, soft or round — for the window and the widgets alike.
- The settings show the line widget as it looks now.

## 0.6.3
- A record page of the big public archive opens as its download folder.
- One file per chapter: copies in other bit rates or codecs and a whole-book file beside the chapters are left out.

## 0.6.2
- Counted nouns take the language's own forms: one, a few, many.

## 0.6.1
- Sealed folders: a node's key may come in several handle:key pairs; each is tried and the one that opens the name is kept. A key that opens nothing is reported as such instead of "no sound files".

## 0.6
- Shared folders whose key travels in the link: names, file keys and the files themselves are decrypted on the phone; a broken fetch goes on from where it stopped, a cover is read from the head of the first chapter. Both forms of such a link work, and a link to one file is a book of one chapter.

## 0.5
- A link is taken out of whatever text it came in: quotes, line breaks, words around it and punctuation stuck to it are left behind; a folder of a known kind wins over a plain web address. "Paste" puts only the link into the field.

## 0.4
- Two more kinds of link: a public disk folder read through its open listing service, and a plain web folder — a server page that lists its files (a single sound file's address works too). Plain-text addresses are allowed for servers at home.
- Links of kinds not read yet are recognised and answered with "not supported yet" instead of "not a link".

## 0.3
- One-cell widget: play or pause, with a ring of how far the book has been heard.
- Widget background in the settings: solid, 75, 50, 25 percent or none, for every widget.

## 0.2
- Home screen widget: a line (cover, title, chapter, back, play, forward) that becomes a card when made taller; drawn to the exact size of its cell, only when something changes, in the application's theme and language.

## 0.1
- First public version: books from folders on the phone and from public cloud folder links, chapters in numeric order, the place kept per book, shelf as cards or covers, light and dark themes, three languages.
