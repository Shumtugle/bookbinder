Bookbinder

A simple audiobook player for Android. A book is a folder of sound files.

Download the APK Source
Style
Theme
Corners

The same choices as in the app's settings, working on this page. On the phone, “wallpaper colours” are taken from your wallpaper; here a calm violet stands in for them.

The shelf

Open a book: it moves to the top. The bar under each book is the whole book, cut into its chapters.

The player

0:00−0:00

Hold the strip, or drag it: a drum of five-second ticks rises. Near the finger it moves five seconds at a time, further off faster and faster; at an edge it keeps turning. Let go to land there.

Books on the phone

Any folder of sound files under Music/Audiobooks is a book; folders of your own are added once in the system folder picker.

Books from links

Paste or share a public cloud folder link: the book gets its own folder and comes in chapter by chapter, the one being heard first. Nothing is ever deleted by the app.

The place, always

Chapter and second in every book. A book taken up after a pause steps back a few seconds.

Widgets

A line, a card and a single key, drawn in the chosen look, with how transparent their ground is.

Keys

Back 15 s, forward 30 s, chapters, speed, a sleep timer; headset keys and a notification with the same keys.

Three languages

English, Russian and Finnish, chosen inside the app. Android 8 or later.

Java, platform only, no build system. License: 0BSD. What changed: the changelog.
